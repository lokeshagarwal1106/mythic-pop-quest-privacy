Privacy Policy for Mythic Pop Quest

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Mythic Pop Quest is a puzzle game published by Lokesh Agarwal. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Information handled by the game

The game does not require an account or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information. Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to display ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share approximate location derived from an IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or other identifiers. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data is encrypted in transit.

Where required, the game displays Google's consent controls before requesting ads. You can also manage ad personalization and reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

Local game progress can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. Advertising data is handled by Google under Google's policies and retention practices.

Children

The game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
