Privacy Policy

Publisher: Lookesh Aagarwal. Updated: 9 October 2026.

No data collection or tracking

This children's coloring app has no advertising, analytics, login, chat, in-app purchases, or tracking. It does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. Coloring pages are bundled with the app and work offline.

Your artwork

Current coloring is held on your device. Changing pictures or closing the app clears unsaved work. Saving a picture opens Android's system file picker so you can choose where to save the PNG. The app does not require photo-library access or upload artwork itself. If you choose a cloud storage provider through Android's picker, that provider processes your saved file under its own privacy policy.

Children and parents

Children can color without providing personal information. Parents should supervise saving or sharing pictures. The app does not offer public sharing or messaging.
