Privacy Policy

Publisher: Lookesh Aagarwal. Updated: 10 October 2026.

No data collection or tracking

This children's coloring app has no advertising, analytics, login, chat, in-app purchases, or tracking. We do not collect or receive personal data or artwork from the app. Coloring pages are bundled and work offline.

On-device saved pictures

Save picture stores a PNG in My Pictures on your device only. Android uses private app storage; the browser version uses local browser storage. Saved pictures remain when the app closes. You can view or delete them inside My Pictures. Uninstalling the app or clearing app/browser storage removes them. Export pictures you want to keep. Android cloud backup is disabled. Unsaved coloring is lost when changing pictures or closing the app.

Optional sharing and export

After a grown-up check, Share opens Android's share sheet and grants the destination you choose temporary read access to the selected PNG only. Export PNG uses Android's system file picker; the browser version downloads the PNG. These actions occur only at your request. The destination app or cloud provider may handle the picture under its own privacy policy. We do not upload artwork or receive a copy. No photo-library or contacts permission is required.

Children and parents

Children can color and save without providing personal information. Parents should supervise sharing and exporting. The app has no public gallery, account, or in-app messaging. The grown-up check is a local arithmetic prompt; it does not collect age or identity.
