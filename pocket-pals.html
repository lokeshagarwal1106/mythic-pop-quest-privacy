Pocket Pals — Privacy Policy

Updated: 27 September 2026. Advertising builds: version 1.2 (code 3) and version 1.3 (code 4). Developer: Lookesh Aagarwal. Android package: com.pocketpals.app.

Version 1.3: app-entry advertising

In addition to optional rewarded unlocks, version 1.3 may show a dismissible Google App Open ad when you return to the app. It uses consent-gated preloading, skips first use and unavailable ads, and does not wait for an ad before allowing access. Ads are not shown inside the pet overlay; pets are hidden before fullscreen advertising. Google supplies the image or video creative. The SDK data types, purposes, local storage and privacy choices described below also apply to version 1.3. The rewarded-only presentation description below applies specifically to version 1.2.

About Pocket Pals

Pocket Pals displays animated animals above your home screen and other apps. No account is required. The developer does not operate an account database or collect pet preferences on a developer server. This does not mean that the app collects no data: Google advertising and consent SDKs process the data described below.

Saved on your device

Ownership, reward progress, preferences and any legacy balances are stored locally. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local data. Android cloud backup is disabled and app data is excluded from device-transfer rules. Previously unlocked looks stay unlocked when upgrading.

Advertising and consent

Version 1.2 includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). UMP checks whether a privacy message is required on each launch. Advertising initializes and preloads an ad only when UMP permits ad requests. A video is shown only after you tap its unlock button. No banners, automatic interstitials or ads inside the pet overlay are used. You can continue using already-unlocked pets without watching ads. Settings displays Privacy choices when Google requires that entry point.

Google's SDKs collect and share information such as IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), app/ad interactions, diagnostic information including performance and crash information, and device or other identifiers. Purposes include advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google reports that SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. These services may process data outside your country, and Google's policies govern its retention and privacy rights. Test IDs do not disable all SDK data processing. The test build uses Google's official test ads and does not earn publisher revenue.

See Google's Privacy Policy, Google advertising information and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures. You can also manage advertising identifiers in Android settings. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are not integrated.

Permissions and overlays

Display over other apps shows your selected pet. The app handles touches on its pet window but does not read, record or transmit content from underlying apps. Foreground-service and notification permissions support the visible overlay with Pause and Hide controls. Screen and battery events are processed locally to manage animation. Vibration supports optional feedback. Internet and network-state permissions support ads and consent; advertising dependencies may declare advertising-ID, ad-services and wake-lock permissions. No camera, microphone, contacts, location or accessibility-service access is requested.

Sharing, deletion and external links

The developer does not sell personal data. Google SDK processing and sharing are described above. Clearing local app storage does not delete information already processed by Google; use Google's privacy tools for that information. Opening this policy launches your external browser: the browser and GitHub Pages hosting provider can process normal web-request information. Emailing support shares your email address and the message you choose to send with us and email providers. Please avoid sensitive personal information.

Version history and audience

Version 1.0 test builds also included advertising SDKs. Version 1.1 (code 2) was an offline build without those SDKs; its no-collection description does not apply to version 1.2. Pocket Pals is intended for ages 13 and above, not children under 13. Future changes to data practices will be reflected in this policy and the app-store declaration.
