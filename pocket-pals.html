Pocket Pals — Privacy Policy

Effective: 27 September 2026. Developer: Lookesh Aagarwal. Android package: com.pocketpals.app.

About the app

Pocket Pals displays user-selected animated animals over the home screen and other apps. No user account is required. This policy applies to Pocket Pals, not the developer's other games.

Data stored on your device

Pet ownership, earned unlock progress, preferences and legacy in-app balances are stored locally. The app does not operate its own account database or upload those preferences to a developer server. Android backup settings may affect app-data backup. Clearing the app's storage removes its local data; this also resets your unlocks and preferences.

Overlay and device permissions

Display over other apps is used only to show and interact with your chosen pet. The app receives touch input on its pet window; it does not read, record or transmit screen text or content from underlying apps. No accessibility service, microphone, camera, contacts or location permission is requested.

A foreground service and notification keep the user-enabled overlay running and provide Pause and Hide controls. Screen-on/off events suspend animation when the display is off. Internet and network access support advertising and consent; vibration supports touch feedback. The app's advertising dependencies may also declare advertising-identifier, advertising-services and wake-lock permissions.

Advertising and consent

Pocket Pals includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Rewarded videos are requested only after an unlock button is tapped; there are no banner or interstitial placements and no ads in pet overlay windows. The current test build uses Google's test advertising IDs.

Google's SDKs may collect IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions, and diagnostic information such as performance and crash data. Google processes these for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention under its own policies. Test ads do not mean all SDK network communication is disabled. Data can be processed outside your country. Google determines retention for information processed by its services.

Where a consent message is required, UMP displays it before ad requests are allowed. When available, Settings → Privacy choices lets you revisit that message. Android settings also let you manage advertising identifiers. Ads may be unavailable without the required consent or connectivity; the app does not grant an unlock unless an earned-reward callback is received.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are not integrated into this Pocket Pals build.

Sharing, security and retention

The developer does not sell your personal data. Google SDK processing and sharing are governed by Google's policies and your applicable choices. SDK network data is encrypted in transit according to Google's disclosures. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Local app data remains until it is cleared or removed; deletion of local data does not itself erase information already processed by Google.

Children and privacy requests

Pocket Pals is intended for ages 13 and above and is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information or want to ask about access, correction or deletion rights, contact us. We will explain what information the developer controls and, where relevant, direct you to Google's privacy tools.
