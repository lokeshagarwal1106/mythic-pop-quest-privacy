Pocket Pals — Privacy Policy

Effective: 27 September 2026. Applies to version 1.1 (version code 2) and later versions with the same offline design. Developer: Lookesh Aagarwal. Android package: com.pocketpals.app.

An offline companion

Pocket Pals displays user-selected animated animals over the home screen and other apps. The app does not collect or transmit personal data off your device. It has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK or internet permission. It does not sell or share personal data. All animal looks can be unlocked locally without watching ads or paying.

Data kept only on your phone

Pet ownership, selected looks and preferences are saved locally. Existing legacy balances and unlock progress may remain in local storage after upgrading. These are not sent to the developer or another company. Cloud backup is disabled and app data is excluded from Android device-transfer rules. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes this local data and resets your settings and unlocks; there is no server copy to restore.

Permissions and overlays

Display over other apps lets your chosen pet appear over your home screen and other apps. The app handles touches on the pet window; it does not read, record or transmit text or content underneath. A special-use foreground service and its notification keep the overlay running with Pause and Hide controls. Notifications are optional where Android requires permission. Vibration provides optional touch feedback. Screen and battery events help pause animation; they are processed on the device only. No camera, microphone, location, contacts or accessibility-service access is requested.

External links and contacting us

If you choose to open this privacy policy, it opens in your external browser. The browser and this GitHub Pages website's hosting provider may process normal web-request information such as an IP address under their own policies. This is separate from the offline app. If you email support, your email provider and our email provider process the message, and we receive the address and information you choose to send. Do not send sensitive personal information.

Earlier test builds

Version 1.0 test builds included Google's AdMob and UMP SDKs. Those SDKs could process device identifiers, approximate location derived from IP addresses, advertising interactions and diagnostics under Google's Privacy Policy. They have been removed in version 1.1. This change does not erase data that Google may already have processed from an earlier build.

Audience and changes

Pocket Pals is intended for ages 13 and above and is not designed for children under 13. Future changes to data practices will require an updated policy and app-store declaration. The app's offline design does not control data independently handled by Android, Google Play or your browser.
