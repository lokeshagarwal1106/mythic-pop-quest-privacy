Bubble Pop privacy policy for iPhone, iPad and Mac

This policy covers the Apple edition of Bubble Pop: Slingshot Puzzles, version 1.0. Android editions have their own policy. The developer is Lokesh Agarwal.

Your game data

You can play without creating a player account. Progress, scores, collected items, settings and purchase-access state are stored on your device. This edition has no developer-run cloud-save service and no Firebase gameplay analytics collection. We do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

Age and advertising on iPhone and iPad

The app asks for an age once to choose privacy treatment. The number stays in local app storage; the app uses it to set child-directed and under-age-of-consent flags for the advertising SDK. The age screen is not an identity check. Child and teen requests use restrictive privacy treatment. The app requests non-personalized ads and a general-audience content filter.

The iOS edition uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Depending on settings and eligibility, Google's SDK may process IP-based approximate location, app/device identifiers, ad views and interactions, crash information and performance diagnostics for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and SDK operation. Non-personalized advertising can still involve data processing. See Google's privacy policy and Google's iOS SDK data disclosure.

Where required, a regional consent form appears before eligible ad requests. A privacy choice can be reopened from Settings when Google's SDK requires it. Advertising availability depends on the connection, region, age treatment, consent eligibility and inventory. The Mac edition does not include AdMob or the mobile advertising SDK. Purchasing the permanent unlock removes in-app ads.

Purchases

Apple processes payment and maintains the App Store purchase record. The app checks StoreKit purchase status to grant or restore paid levels and ad-free access. It keeps local purchase-access information, including identifiers needed to avoid granting an unapproved pending purchase. We do not receive your card number or bank account details. Purchases, refunds and Ask to Buy approvals are governed by Apple's store processes. See Apple's privacy policy.

Retention and choices

Local game data remains until it is removed from the device. Uninstalling or removing application data can erase progress; a device backup may retain a copy. Restore purchase can recover an eligible App Store unlock but does not recover your local game progress. Apple and Google manage their own records under their policies. You can manage device privacy settings and request help at the contact below.

Support and this website

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to answer your request. Do not send passwords, verification codes, full payment details or unnecessary personal information. This help site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may process ordinary access logs; it does not add advertising or analytics scripts.
