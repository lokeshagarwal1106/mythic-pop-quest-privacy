Bubble Pop Apple edition support

Aim the slingshot, time a shot and clear matching moving targets. This help page covers Bubble Pop: Slingshot Puzzles for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Level 1 is free. A single non-consumable App Store purchase unlocks Levels 2–100,000 and removes in-app ads. There is no subscription. Check the local price and terms in Apple's payment sheet before confirming.

Play and controls

On iPhone or iPad, tap a target or drag to aim and release to shoot. On Mac, use the mouse or trackpad. Use Pause before stepping away. Levels are generated stages; the catalog is not 100,000 individually handcrafted puzzles.

Restore an unlock

  1. Use the Apple Account that originally purchased the unlock.
  2. Connect to the internet and open Settings → Restore purchase.
  3. Complete Apple's authentication if requested. A pending Ask to Buy request grants access after approval.

Restore recovers eligible paid access, not device-only scores or progress. If a purchase is refunded or revoked, paid access can end.

Ads and privacy

Ads are available in the iPhone/iPad edition where permitted and supplied. They are not included in the native Mac edition. The permanent unlock removes ads. You do not need to tap an ad to continue playing the free level. Optional ad rewards are granted only when the ad SDK reports that the reward was earned. Advertising privacy choices appear in Settings when required. Read the Apple edition privacy policy.

Compatibility

The Apple build requires iOS/iPadOS 16.4 or later, or macOS 13.3 or later. iPhone 11 and newer models that run a supported iOS version are within the configured device range. The native Mac build includes Intel and Apple Silicon support. An OS version meeting the minimum does not guarantee identical performance on every device. Keep your OS and app updated.

Purchase or performance help

Contact techhytech366@gmail.com with your app version, device model, OS version, the affected level and a short description. A screenshot can help; hide private payment or account information first. Do not send passwords, verification codes or bank/card details. Refund requests are handled through Apple's purchase support.
